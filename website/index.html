Team 4154R

On VEX team 4154R, I write the autonomous routines and driver-control code in C++, using PROS and EZ-Template, and I author the engineering notebook that judges read. I started as a coder for team 4154S, then moved to 4154R as primary programmer.

The team won the South Florida State Championship, took a Regional Triple Crown, and qualified for the VEX World Championship in 2026. The work that mattered most was making autonomous routines consistent, not just impressive once.
