Team 4154R

VEX robotics is where code meets the field. From autonomous routines to driver control and the engineering notebook, our team turned iteration into a state championship and a 2026 Worlds qualification.

Eyes for Agents

A bridge between cameras and AI agents. It turns camera events into descriptions using a local vision model, helping an agent understand what is happening in the physical world. Our team earned second place at the Miami eMerge AI Hackathon.

CubeSat

A 1U CubeSat model built as part of an Engineering and Design Fellowship. Its Arduino Nano collects readings from temperature, humidity, light, and motion sensors and saves data to an SD card; the solar panels are simulated.
