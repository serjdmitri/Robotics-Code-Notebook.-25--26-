The question

A satellite in miniature.

How can a small model bring the parts of a satellite-like system together for hands-on learning?

The takeaway

See the system together.

The model brings sensors, a microcontroller, storage, and a 3D-printed structure together in one compact build.

A miniature system

A miniature system.

An Arduino Nano reads temperature, humidity, light, and motion sensors and saves the data to an SD card.

Designed to learn

Designed to learn.

The model uses a 3D-printed structure and USB power. Its solar panels are simulated for educational purposes.

The story

Inside the model

The 1U structure contains an Arduino Nano, temperature and humidity sensor, light sensor, motion sensor, SD card module, and status LEDs. The sensors collect environmental readings and the Arduino saves the data.

The story

A platform for learning

The model uses USB power and decorative simulated solar panels. Its 3D-printed frame brings the parts together as an accessible way to explore a satellite-like system.
