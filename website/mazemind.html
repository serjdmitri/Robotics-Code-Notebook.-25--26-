The question

Make learning visible.

What if you could see an AI learn instead of only reading about it? MazeMind makes the reward and the maze part of the experience.

The takeaway

Change the rewards.

The mouse learns from the rewards it receives. Changing the maze or reward scheme lets you explore how those choices affect learning.

Train an AI

Train an AI.

Design or generate a maze, choose a reward scheme, and watch a Q-learning agent learn by trial and error. Track its progress as it trains.

Become the AI

Become the AI.

Take the mouse’s place and solve the maze yourself. Walls reveal themselves as you encounter them, so you experience the puzzle from the agent’s perspective.

The story

Choose the reward

MazeMind offers guided learning, self-discovery, and multiple-path reward schemes. You can build or generate a maze, adjust learning and exploration, and follow results such as success rate and learning curves.

The story

Runs in your browser

The project is built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with Q-learning implemented in the browser. Saved agents and leaderboard times live in local browser storage. The README credits a four-person team.
